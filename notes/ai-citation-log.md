# AI Assistance and Citation Log

IB academic integrity policy requires disclosing AI tool use on this IA. Fill this in yourself, in your own words, each time you use an AI tool. Don't let the tool write its own entries, the record needs to reflect what you actually asked for and what you did with what came back.

Check this format against your school's actual required disclosure format before relying on it, this is a reasonable starting structure, not an official template.
